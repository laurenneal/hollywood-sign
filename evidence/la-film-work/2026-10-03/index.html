How to read the comparison

Keep the measures separate

The default compares each latest observation with the same month or quarter in 2019. That helps align the seasonal calendar, but is not a seasonal adjustment. Comparing different months or quarters can include seasonal effects.

Percentage change = (later value − earlier value) ÷ earlier value × 100. A percentage change cannot be calculated from a zero starting value. Calculations use unrounded observations; percentages are displayed to one decimal place. “Source vintage” identifies the date or version of the source used. Historical figures may include revisions made after their measurement period.

Payroll jobs, people, permitted shoot days, and soundstage occupancy are different measures. Neither chart estimates actor income, unpaid work, worker sentiment, or the effect of a particular policy. Source coverage and the selected comparison affect the interpretation.

Payroll jobs: Source values in thousands of jobs multiplied by 1,000; no seasonal adjustment or smoothing.

On-location shoot days: Reported total shoot days, unchanged. Q2 2026 is carried as preliminary because the local feed uses the web update.
