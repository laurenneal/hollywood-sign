# The Hollywood SIGN

**SIGN = Shoot days, Income, Grosses, News.** A drawn map of a fictional studio lot where every crowd, queue,
lamp and weather state is bound to one public number. Real numbers, drawn in code. No generative AI image
model anywhere. Hover over anything and it shows you the data source.

Live: **https://laurenneal.github.io/hollywood-sign/**

This repository is the published site only: the scene, the data it reads, and the source tables. It is
regenerated by a scheduled job every two hours (data) and on every change to the scene; the history is one
commit deep on purpose. The feeds, tests and documentation live in a separate repository that is private for
now and will be opened.

- [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md): every signal, its source, series id, cadence, licence and the evidence quote.
- [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md): licence terms for everything under `data/`. The compilation is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `data/signals.json`: the contract the scene reads; `data/history/*.csv`: every value with its vintage.

Attributions: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (public domain); Wikipedia, "List of <year> box office number-one
films in the United States" (CC BY-SA 4.0; figures cite Box Office Mojo); Netflix Top 10 derived aggregates only
(netflix.com/tudum/top10 — no raw Tudum tables); FilmLA (cite FilmLA for every shoot-day figure); GDELT Project
(trade-press tone); California EDD WARN reports; counts derived from the public RSS feeds of Deadline, Variety,
The Hollywood Reporter, IndieWire and TheWrap (banner/newsstand only — not sky climate). No Hollywood Sign
imagery; no real studio marks; the lot is fictional. A Fair Play Films project: https://fairplayfilms.com/
