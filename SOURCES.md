# Data sources

Generated from `feeds/*.manifest.json` by `scripts/gen_data_sources.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Every number on the page traces to one row here. **Redistributable** is the verdict for this
repository: `yes` means derived numbers may live in `data/`, `derived-only` means aggregates with
citation and no raw rows, `no` means the feed may only run locally (`feeds/local/`) and never
writes into `data/`. A CI gate (`scripts/validate_manifests.py`) enforces the last case.

Nothing here is legal advice; each verdict is a reading of the published terms on the date the
evidence quote was captured.
