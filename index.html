02 / About SIGN

For the people
who work
in film.

SIGN is a film-industry research project by Lauren Neal, an actor and data scientist.

People working in film need usable information about an industry that determines whether they can make a living. SIGN brings together public data and source documents so workers, filmmakers, and writers can check claims and use the figures themselves.

The longer-term work is about access to jobs and the concentration of power in the industry. This first report covers LA employment and location filming.
